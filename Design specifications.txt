Website Design Specification for Pritom Mondal

Based on the provided reference UI ("image_71481b.jpg"), this document outlines the structure, content, and styling guidelines for a clean, minimalist, black-and-white portfolio website, populated with information from Pritom Mondal's resume.

Global Styling Guidelines

Color Palette: Strictly Monochrome (Black, White, and shades of Grey).

Typography:

Headings: Large, bold, sans-serif font (e.g., Helvetica, Inter, or a similar modern grotesque). High contrast.

Body Text: Smaller, clean sans-serif for high readability.

Caps: Extensive use of ALL CAPS for navigation, section headers, and stylistic elements.

Layout:

Whitespace-heavy, grid-based layout.

Distinct horizontal sections separating content.

Use of delicate, thin lines (borders) to define sections and UI elements.

UI Elements:

Pill-shaped (rounded-full) buttons and tags.

Circular icon buttons (specifically with directional arrows).

Subtle hover effects (e.g., inverting colors from black-on-white to white-on-black).
